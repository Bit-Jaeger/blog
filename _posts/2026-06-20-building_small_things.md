## /where am I coming from?
I am in my second semester right now and it is one that is pretty filled with maths, theoretical stuff and alsomst no coding at all. This is my standpoint right now and I did not like it. The semester is quite stresfull though so I really do have to take the time and cut away other stuff to be able to code a little. Don't get me wrong: I still like my modules and I do have a lot of fun in getting better in statistics or algorithms. I just don't like that I am not creating code.

## /why?
I still found a lot of fun in getting more routine for coding and just sitting down, reading man-pages, getting to know python or in particular customtkinter a little more. Of course I am nowhere near somebody that you could call "good" computer scientist. But nonetheless I did find myself in wanting to go to bed and still thinking about a small project I started. I was thinking about a solution, sat down and found myself at my desk an hour later. <br> 
And I do think a big part of this is that I particularly picked out small(!) projects that do fit the timeslot I do have for them pretty well. 

## /what project to pick?
That is why I am posting this in the first place. I tried picking projects that are not too big. I picked them with the intent of being able to finish them in max 2 weeks in my freetime. I did not want to start the development of a new game that would take me probably years to finish. <br>
Sure I would learn a LOT from that as well. But the danger of it being and abandoned repo in 4 weeks would probably be close to 100% if I am being honest with myself. 

## /where is the benefit?
This is exactly where the benefit lies for me. Picking a small project, peeking into something new, that gets my attention is just perfect. I am not too frustrated and the problems I run into along the way are not too hard to solve but I still benefit from them - I am actively learning. <br>
For me it just seems quite perfect to pick things, that I know I can finish in a week or two - at least during this stressfull semester. I do have some bigger things in mind but I am purposely postponing these to the end of the year where I should find more time to tackle these!

## /projects that worked for me
- qr code generator in python using customtkinter
- webpages that are not too spectacular
- some cli-program for Linux that just does a small thing (monitoring system load)

<br>
maybe these projects do grab your attention as well or you do get a better imagination about the size of them. <br>
Thanks for your patience and have a nice time! Enjoy the process of struggling :)
