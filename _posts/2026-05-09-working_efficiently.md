## Time
Since I am getting older it seems like time is running faster day by day. Weeks go by so frequently that a month is not what it used to be anymore. <br>
While I don't want to be sad about it i occasionally do think about this changing over time. If I think about the holidays while in primary school or one year of school this took so much longer than any year after I turned 18. At least that's how I feel like.
## Actively thinking about what to do
Because time seems to be rushing nonstop I am actively contemplating on what to use my time for. I am not living into the day anymore but rather trying to plan everything out. After years of trial and error I eventually found the method that works best for me. However I still don't find nearly enough time to do everything I want to do. I just feel like I am always a little bit behind and loosing the sight of being 'in time' with everything I want to do.
